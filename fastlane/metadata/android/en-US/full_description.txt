FITNS is a fitness tracker that keeps everything on your phone. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no trackers.

<b>Train</b>
<ul>
<li>Log workouts with sets, reps, weight and how hard each set felt</li>
<li>A rest timer that alerts you even with the screen locked</li>
<li>Rep counting for push-ups, jumps and squats</li>
<li>Reusable routines, ready-made ones to start from, and programs that cycle through your training days</li>
<li>Tell FITNS what equipment a place has, and routines swap exercises to fit</li>
<li>Low-impact mode, warm-ups, cool-downs and a "short on time" trim</li>
</ul>

<b>Move</b>
<ul>
<li>Step counting with daily, weekly and monthly totals</li>
<li>Optional all-day counting and a home-screen widget</li>
</ul>

<b>Eat</b>
<ul>
<li>Calories and macros, a built-in food list and your own foods</li>
<li>Offline barcode scanning for packaged food</li>
<li>A calorie plan for your goal, with a weekly check-in that follows your real weight trend</li>
</ul>

<b>Track</b>
<ul>
<li>Weight, BMI, measurements, body-fat estimate and progress photos</li>
<li>Water, sleep and optional cycle tracking</li>
<li>Streaks, monthly summaries, muscle balance, personal records and badges</li>
<li>Reminders for workouts, water and weigh-ins</li>
</ul>

<b>Yours</b>
<ul>
<li>Back up everything to one file and restore it on a new phone</li>
<li>Delete all data any time</li>
<li>Open source under the MIT License</li>
</ul>
