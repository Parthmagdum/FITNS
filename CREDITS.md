# Picture credits

Every picture bundled with FITNS is freely licensed. The app has no
internet permission, so the pictures ship inside it (`assets/`), resized
and converted to WebP. The same list is in the app under
About → Picture credits.

## Exercise photos — `assets/exercises/`

From [free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db) by
yuhonas and contributors, released into the **public domain** (Unlicense).
Each built-in exercise with a faithful match has its start (`_0`) and
finish (`_1`) photo, padded to 4:3. The match for each exercise is
`exercisePhotoCounts` in `lib/data/image_assets.dart`; exercises not listed
there keep their icon.
