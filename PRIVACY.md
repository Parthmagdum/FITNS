# FITNS privacy policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

FITNS is an offline fitness tracker made by Parth Magdum (TRAKVRS). This
policy covers the FITNS Android app.

## The short version

FITNS does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you
enter stays on your phone.

## What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, routines, steps, food and water logs, body measurements,
progress photos, sleep logs, cycle dates and settings are stored only in
the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read them.

FITNS has no internet permission, so it can't send this data anywhere —
not to us, not to anyone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash
reporting or third-party trackers.

## Permissions

FITNS asks for each of these only when you use the feature that needs it:

- **Physical activity** — to read your phone's step counter.
- **Camera** — to scan barcodes on food packets. Images are not saved.
- **Notifications** — for the rest timer, step counting and reminders.
- **Alarms & reminders** — so the rest timer alert arrives on time.
- **Run at startup** — to restart step counting and reminders after the
  phone restarts.

Progress photos are taken with your camera app or picked from your
gallery through Android's own picker; FITNS keeps a copy in its private
storage.

## Backups

If you make a backup, FITNS saves it as a file wherever you choose
(for example, your Downloads folder or a cloud drive app). Where that
file goes after that is up to you and the app you saved it with.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → **Delete all data** erases everything from the app.
Uninstalling FITNS also removes all of its data from your phone.

## Children

FITNS is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from
anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a
new date.
