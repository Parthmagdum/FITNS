# FITNS logo options

Four concepts for a new FITNS logo. **Number 4, Lift-off, was picked** and
is the FITNS logo from 0.1.22 (drawn by tool/make_icons.py); the others are
kept here for reference.

| | Concept | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Constellation | An F drawn as points joined by lines: days tracked, joined into a picture of progress. |
| 2 | Staircase | A flight of stairs climbing to the right, a dot on the top step: getting there, a step a day. |
| 3 | Plate | A weight plate seen face on, its grip slot cut as an F. |
| 4 | Lift-off | A slanted F whose top bar turns into an arrow, climbing up and away. |

- `fitns-logo-options.png`: all four, big and at launcher sizes on light
  and dark.
- `fitns-logo-<n>-<name>.png`: each one alone, 512 px.
- `make_logo_options.py`: draws them (run from the repo root:
  `python docs/logo-options/make_logo_options.py docs/logo-options`).

They were designed here from scratch, but no trademark or logo search has
been done. Check one before relying on any of them as a brand mark.
