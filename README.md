# FITNS

An offline, open-source fitness tracker for Android. Everything you log
stays on your phone: the app has no internet permission.

## Features

- **Steps**: daily, weekly and monthly totals from the phone's step counter,
  with optional all-day counting and a home-screen widget
- **Workouts**: exercises, sets, reps, weight, effort per set and a rest
  timer that alerts you on a locked phone; rep counting for push-ups, jumps
  and squats
- **Routines and programs**: reusable routines, ready-made ones to start
  from, and programs that cycle through your training days
- **Places**: tell FITNS what equipment you have, and routines swap
  exercises to fit; a low-impact mode skips jumps and hard landings
- **Food and water**: calories and macros, a built-in food list, your own
  foods, and offline barcode scanning for packaged food
- **Goal and plan**: a calorie target for your goal, with a weekly check-in
  that adjusts it to your real weight trend
- **Body**: weight, BMI, measurements, a body-fat estimate and progress
  photos
- **Sleep** logging and optional **cycle tracking**
- **Progress**: streaks, monthly summaries, muscle balance, personal
  records and badges
- **Reminders** for workouts, water and weigh-ins
- **Backup and restore** to a single file you keep wherever you like

## Privacy

FITNS collects nothing. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers,
and the app can't reach the internet. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## Install

Download the latest APK from
[Releases](https://github.com/Parthmagdum/FITNS/releases). Most phones
want `fitns-arm64-v8a.apk`.
